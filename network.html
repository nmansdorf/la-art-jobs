Pre-filtered 2nd-degree searches + verified art leadership at every studio with a live opening · August 8, 2026

How to use this — read once, then it's self-explanatory

Every blue pill below is a live LinkedIn search URL pre-filtered to 1st- and 2nd-degree connections only. Click it while logged in and LinkedIn returns exactly the people in your extended network at that company. Because degree is relative to whoever is signed in, Nick and Monica will get different results from the same link — run them from both accounts and compare. Monica's own network is the more useful one for warm intros; Nick's is the wider net for referrals.

  1. Open a link. Confirm the Connections filter shows 1st + 2nd (it's pre-set in the URL).
  2. Anyone marked 2nd — click through and look at "Mutual connections." That shared person is the actual asset, not the target.
  3. Ask the mutual for the intro. Never cold-InMail a 2nd-degree when a mutual exists.
  4. The named people in each table are verified from public sources — search those names first, they're the highest-value hits.

Where to actually meet these people

LightBox Expo 2026 — Oct 23–25, Pasadena Convention Center

The event for this exact target. Sponsors include Sony Pictures Animation, Netflix Animation, Disney, Pixar, DreamWorks. Artists sell at tables and the norm is that working professionals expect to talk to attendees — it's the least awkward networking environment in the industry. The Concept Art Awards ceremony runs inside it on Oct 24.

Note: the official site lists Oct 23–25 while a Visit Pasadena release says Oct 24–26. Trust the official site and confirm at purchase. lightboxexpo.com

CTN Animation Expo — Nov 19–22, 2026, Burbank Airport Marriott

"Career Camp" — directors review portfolios, sign-ups are onsite only, no advance registration. Arrive early on day one. This is the more career-services-oriented of the two LA animation cons; LightBox is more community/inspiration. Early bird $149 for 3 days, $59 single day. ctn-events.com/ctnx2026

Gallery Nucleus, Alhambra — check monthly

Not a conference, but arguably the highest networking density per hour in LA animation art. Hosts studio art-book launches and panels — including The Art of KPop Demon Hunters exhibition with the Sony Pictures Animation team. Openings are free or cheap and the artists are physically in the room. gallerynucleus.com/events

The rest, ranked

  • TAG Local 839 (Burbank) — Sony Pictures Animation runs its visdev apprenticeships through the Guild. A named pathway that bypasses the cold-portfolio pile. Public calendar
  • XDS (External Development Summit) — where Riot's Ben Skutt speaks. Less crowded than GDC and disproportionately populated by the people who commission concept art. xdsummit.com
  • Women in Animation mentorship program — multiple application rounds per year, LA-headquartered. Round 1 2026 has passed; watch for the next. womeninanimation.org
  • Women in Games — free monthly online socials and a free global Careers Expo. womeningames.org
  • ArtStation Challenges — studio-sponsored, judged by working art directors, winners get real visibility. artstation.com/challenges
  • GDC 2027 — Mar 1–5, 2027, Moscone Center. Historically hosts studio portfolio reviews. Relevant for the games half of the list. GDC 2026 has passed.
  • Riot Worlds Artist Alley — Nov 12–14, 2026, NYC. Free to apply but the deadline was Aug 7, 2026 (yesterday). Flag for 2027.
