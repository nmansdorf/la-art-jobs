Pre-filtered 2nd-degree searches + verified art leadership at every studio with a live opening · Updated September 12, 2026

How to use this — read once, then it's self-explanatory

Every blue pill below is a live LinkedIn search URL pre-filtered to 1st- and 2nd-degree connections only. Click it while logged in and LinkedIn returns exactly the people in your extended network at that company. Because degree is relative to whoever is signed in, Nick and Monica will get different results from the same link — run them from both accounts and compare. Monica's own network is the more useful one for warm intros; Nick's is the wider net for referrals.

  1. Open a link. Confirm the Connections filter shows 1st + 2nd (it's pre-set in the URL).
  2. Anyone marked 2nd — click through and look at "Mutual connections." That shared person is the actual asset, not the target.
  3. Ask the mutual for the intro. Never cold-InMail a 2nd-degree when a mutual exists.
  4. The named people in each table are verified from public sources — search those names first, they're the highest-value hits.

The calendar — updated September 12

Watch this weekly: the LightBox portfolio review lottery

Still not announced as of today. The page continues to read only "Check back here for details on the 2026 Portfolio Review Lottery." Based on prior years — the 2024 lottery closed ~Sept 20, the 2025 one opened ~Oct 2 — expect it to open between late September and the first week of October. That's within the next one to three weeks.

There is a trap here. Search results surface a LightBox Threads post reading "The Portfolio Review Lottery is now open!" that looks current. Its post ID decodes to October 2025. An identical X post dates to October 2024, and an AnimaWorker listing with a September 20 deadline is from 2024. Do not act on any of them.

Two structural details have held every year: reviews are badge-holders only, and each review day needs the matching single-day or 3-day badge. Buy the 3-day badge now so she's eligible the moment the lottery opens. lightboxexpo.com/portfolio-reviews

Illustration West 65 — open now, closes Oct 31

SILA's annual juried competition, and the jurors are working art directors. There is an Entertainment & Visual Development category that is a direct fit, plus Unpublished & Self-Promotional for personal work.

Two details worth knowing. First, most categories require work created or published November 2025 – October 2026, but visual development work may be up to two years old — a much wider window that likely makes older portfolio pieces eligible. Second, entry is $35 for non-members versus $20 for members, and the exhibition fee if accepted is $55 versus $35 — so SILA membership pays for itself at roughly two to three entries. illustrationwest.org/65

Society of Illustrators of Los Angeles — still the highest-value membership

SILA runs genuine portfolio reviews — 15-minute one-on-one sessions with working professionals — and eligibility runs through Illustration West entry or membership. Their public events calendar currently reads "no upcoming events," which contradicts the Sketch Lounge listed elsewhere on the site; the calendar appears stale rather than accurate. Email info@si-la.org or call 800-799-6368 to ask directly when the next review is. si-la.org

The BRIC Foundation — mentorship infrastructure

LA nonprofit doing education and workforce development for entertainment artists: bootcamps, a California apprenticeship pathway for entertainment/media/tech, career readiness, animation grants, a speaker series. It's formally tied to LightBox Expo, and BRC's Edward Hodge has a public profile there — which makes it a warm route to a hiring VP. bricfoundation.org

Gallery Nucleus, Alhambra — check monthly

Not a conference, but arguably the highest networking density per hour in LA animation art. Studio art-book launches and panels, free or cheap, artists physically in the room. gallerynucleus.com/events
