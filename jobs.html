Context you should know

The market, honestly

This is a thin market and the list above is short because it's accurate, not because the search was shallow. My agents checked 50+ studios. Disney Animation, Pixar, Nickelodeon, Warner Bros. Animation, Paramount Animation, DreamWorks, Blizzard, Naughty Dog, Insomniac, Activision, 2K and Scopely currently have zero open 2D concept/design roles.

  • Games in SoCal are still cutting. Riot cut ~80 from the 2XKO team (Feb 2026), Wildlight was gutted (Feb 2026), Lightspeed LA cut ~80 (Jul 2026). Over half of global games layoffs have been in California.
  • Animation has one real tailwind. California's expanded film/TV tax credit added animated features and series for the first time — $6.6B in first-year production spend, ~35,000 jobs. Track the California Film Commission credit award announcements; those projects are legally committed to LA production and are the clearest leading indicator of who staffs up next.
  • The Paramount–Warner Bros. Discovery close (expected Q3 2026) is the biggest near-term risk to LA animation headcount — analysts project 6,000+ cuts.

The union question — the single highest-leverage move

The best-paying LA animation roles (Skydance, Sony Pictures Animation, Netflix Animation) are all covered by The Animation Guild, Local 839. Important: you do not join first and then look for work — it's the reverse.

  • Apply and get hired at a signatory studio as a non-member. Membership is not required to apply.
  • After 30 days of employment, you're required to join.
  • Initiation fee = two weeks' minimum scale (~$5,200 for a journey Designer at 2026 rates) + $100 IATSE fee. No-interest payment plans available.
  • Journey-level scale as of Aug 2, 2026: $64.94/hr, $2,597.60/week — that's the floor, not the market rate.
  • Once in: the members-only jobs email, the TAG Availability Grid, and the #looking-for-job Discord (email Comms@tag839.org) are where a lot of work actually circulates. animationguild.org/looking-for-work

A heads-up on the Netflix "Ink" roles

All four Netflix Animation Ink/Inkubator postings explicitly require "familiarity with AI-assisted creative workflows" and experience "integrating both traditional and generative AI tools." Depending on where she stands on that, it's either a differentiator worth leaning into or a reason to skip — worth deciding before she spends time on the application.
