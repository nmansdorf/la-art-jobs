What changed since August 8

41% of the August list is already dead

Twelve of the twenty-nine roles verified on August 8 had closed by September 12 — five weeks. That decay rate is the single most useful thing this tracker measures: assume any listing older than about four weeks is gone until an application form proves otherwise.

A methodological note that matters if you ever re-check this yourself: five of the dead postings still rendered complete, working application forms. Skydance's Lever pages and Sony's Greenhouse pages both serve fully-functional stale detail pages with live Apply buttons. The only reliable tests are the ATS APIs — api.lever.co/v0/postings/<company>?mode=json and boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/<token>/jobs/<id>, which 404 cleanly on dead reqs. Checking the rendered page alone would have produced a 60% false-positive rate.

Conversely, Greenhouse's board index is stale-cached in the other direction — Sony's Story Artist role is live but doesn't appear in their own board listing. Absence from a board is not proof of death either.

The good news: Disney Television Animation is hiring

DTVA posted three art leads on the same day, August 31 — Character Design, Background Paint, and Location Design — all in Glendale, all at $74.68–$82.15/hr, all asking 5–7 years. That's a coordinated staffing wave for a new series and the best-matched cluster to her profile that has appeared in either sweep. Treat the three as a single application push.

Riot also added a Senior Illustration Artist for Riftbound at $150,000–$209,600 against a 6+ year bar — the first time a role at that pay level has been inside her experience band rather than above it.

Context you should know

The market, honestly

Still thin. Across both sweeps, 50+ studios checked: Walt Disney Animation, Pixar, Nickelodeon, Warner Bros. Animation, Paramount Animation, DreamWorks, Blizzard, Naughty Dog, Insomniac, Activision, Bungie, 2K and Scopely have zero open 2D concept roles. Every indexed concept requisition at Activision and Blizzard returns HTTP 410 Gone.

  • The Paramount–Warner Bros. Discovery merger did not close — it's frozen until a March 2027 antitrust trial. Twelve state attorneys general and the WGA sued; Paramount agreed to pause. This is the biggest correction to the August picture. The projected job cuts (LA County estimates 4,500 local film/TV jobs, up to 15,567 overall) are deferred, not cancelled. Practical read: Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network and Nickelodeon aren't cutting this autumn, but merger limbo produces hiring freezes rather than layoffs — treat all three as paralysed rather than dangerous, and spend the energy elsewhere.
  • No new major LA layoffs were announced between August 8 and September 12. An unusually quiet five weeks. The most recent cuts all pre-date the window: Disney/Pixar (~150 at Pixar, July 21), Sony Pictures (April), Bad Robot (April, relocating to New York), Riot (26 in April, ~80 in February).
  • Pixar's structural shift is worth internalising. Future features are capped at 16,500 person-weeks of labour, down from 18,500–20,000+. That's a permanent 15–20% headcount reduction per film at the most prestigious animation employer in the country, and peers benchmark against it.
  • The tax credit remains the one real tailwind. Animated projects with confirmed funded LA production: DONKEY (DreamWorks), Hexed (Disney), an untitled Pixar feature, STEWIE (20th Television Animation), President Curtis (Adult Swim) and The Simpsons Movie 2. No new awards since August 10, and that round was all live-action. Those six titles are the clearest list of who will staff up next.

The union question — the single highest-leverage move

The best-paying LA animation roles (Skydance, Sony Pictures Animation, Netflix Animation) are all covered by The Animation Guild, Local 839. Important: you do not join first and then look for work — it's the reverse.

  • Apply and get hired at a signatory studio as a non-member. Membership is not required to apply.
  • After 30 days of employment, you're required to join.
  • Initiation fee = two weeks' minimum scale (~$5,200 for a journey Designer at 2026 rates) + $100 IATSE fee. No-interest payment plans available.
  • Journey-level scale as of Aug 2, 2026: $64.94/hr, $2,597.60/week — that's the floor, not the market rate.
  • New in September: DreamWorks remote workers ratified their first TAG contract, winning wage and benefit parity with onsite staff. That follows Netflix feature animators earlier in 2026 — the Guild is expanding fast, and the 2024–27 Master Agreement expires in 2027.
  • Once in: the members-only jobs email, the TAG Availability Grid, and the #looking-for-job Discord (email Comms@tag839.org) are where a lot of work actually circulates. animationguild.org/looking-for-work
